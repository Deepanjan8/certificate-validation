PDF Signature Verifier

Cryptographically verifies digital signatures on Indian government PDFs (e‑Aadhaar, e‑PAN, ITR‑V, DigiLocker, e‑Gazette, eSign and other DSC‑signed documents) against the official CCA India trust chain.

🔒 Everything below runs locally in this browser tab. Your PDF is never uploaded anywhere — it never leaves your device. The only network activity this page ever makes is fetching the two public CCA India root certificates directly from cca.gov.in so it can check who ultimately vouches for your document's signature.
Verification logic: PKCS#7 / CMS SignedData parsed with pkijs, hashed against the PDF's own ByteRange, chained to CCA India's published root certificates. No password is required — the signature itself is never encrypted inside these PDFs, even when the document body is.
